lpg_price.csv: REAL. 
Monthly average retail price of a 5 kg Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) refill in Lagos State, Nigeria, measured in Nigerian naira (NGN), January 2016 - April 2026 (124 monthly observations, no gaps). 
Source: National Bureau of Statistics (NBS), Monthly LPG (Cooking Gas) Price Watch reports. 
Data manually extracted from the Lagos State row of the relevant price tables in official NBS PDF reports and compiled into a structured monthly time series. 
Prices are recorded directly in naira per refill, without unit conversion or imputation. 
The dataset supports multi-horizon retail price forecasting and analysis of price volatility and forecast uncertainty.
